What we get asked first.

What is Noodle Mind?

An AI that takes your idea and makes it better. You bring the idea. It brings the questions, the pushback and the sharper version.

Does it have ideas for me?

No. It works on yours. The thinking stays human. Noodle Mind makes it sharper.

Who is it for?

Anyone with an idea and no one to test it on. Founders, teams, students, writers, makers.

What happens to my ideas?

They stay yours. Nothing you type trains anything for anyone else, and you can delete it when you are done.

Is it a chatbot?

It talks, but it is built for one job. It will not write your essay or plan your holiday. It will make your idea better.
